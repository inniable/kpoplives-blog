Latin America's biggest festival stages are opening wide for 5th-generation K-Pop. **SM Entertainment's RIIZE** has been officially confirmed for the **opening day (March 26, 2027)** of Mexico's powerhouse music festival ***Tecate Pa'l Norte***—standing as the **sole K-Pop act** on the primary lineup.

### Beyond Coachella and Lollapalooza

Held annually in Monterrey, *Tecate Pa'l Norte* draws hundreds of thousands of passionate fans across rock, Latin pop, electronic, and alternative music. For years, K-Pop's Western festival push centered heavily on U.S. events like Coachella and Lollapalooza. RIIZE's booking marks a deliberate pivot toward **Latin America**, where K-Pop streaming growth and concert enthusiasm rank among the highest in the world.

### Built for Outdoor Festival Energy

With guitar-driven funk hooks (*"Get A Guitar,"* *"Boom Boom Bass"*) and crisp, athletic choreography, RIIZE's "emotional pop" catalog translates naturally to open-air festival crowds. Performing as the sole K-Pop representative on opening night gives the group an unmatched opportunity to win over tens of thousands of new Latin American listeners live.

**RIIZE** llevará el K-Pop de quinta generación a uno de los escenarios más masivos de Latinoamérica. El grupo de SM Entertainment fue confirmado para el **26 de marzo de 2027**, día inaugural de ***Tecate Pa'l Norte*** en Monterrey, México, como el **único representante del K-Pop** en el cartel principal. Con su sonido vibrante de guitarras y bajos, el grupo tiene la oportunidad perfecta de conquistar a cientos de miles de asistentes mexicanos y latinoamericanos.
