A major Korea–Japan pop crossover is heading into December's year-end television spotlight. **Pledis Entertainment** officially confirmed that **SEVENTEEN's Jeonghan** and Japanese singer-actor **Ryosuke Yamada** are teaming up for a collaborative single titled **"Afterglow,"** scheduled for release on **December 14, 2026**.

### Prime-Time Launch at the FNS Music Festival

Rather than relying purely on a digital drop, the rollout centers on one of Japan's most-watched year-end broadcasts. Jeonghan and Yamada will debut **"Afterglow"** live on **December 9** at Fuji TV's prestigious ***FNS Music Festival***—giving both fanbases a high-profile television moment five days before the single hits streaming platforms.

### Why K-Pop × J-Pop Duets Are Reshaping Asian Pop

SEVENTEEN already ranks among the top-selling K-Pop acts in Japan, while Ryosuke Yamada commands massive mainstream recognition across J-Pop, film, and television drama. Pairing the two artists reflects an evolving regional playbook:

- **Beyond localized Japanese versions:** Instead of merely re-recording Korean hits in Japanese, agencies are co-creating original cross-border singles designed from day one for dual-market television and streaming charts.
- **Year-end momentum:** Premiering on a heritage Japanese broadcast maximizes mainstream household discovery beyond core idol fandoms.
- **Vocal chemistry:** Fans of both artists are already anticipating how Jeonghan's delicate, airy tone will blend with Yamada's dramatic pop delivery.

With December's broadcast just weeks away, *"Afterglow"* is poised to become one of the signature Korea–Japan pop moments closing out 2026.

Una gran colaboración entre Corea y Japón marcará el cierre musical de 2026. **Pledis Entertainment** confirmó que **Jeonghan de SEVENTEEN** y la estrella japonesa **Ryosuke Yamada** lanzarán el sencillo conjunto **"Afterglow"** el próximo **14 de diciembre**.
