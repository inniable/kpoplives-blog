When South Korea crossed 20 million annual foreign visitors earlier this month, tourism planners faced a welcome new challenge: how to turn fast-paced K-Pop and skincare shoppers into **longer-staying cultural and wellness travelers**. From **November 23 to 27, 2026**, the **Korea Tourism Organization (KTO)** and retail giant **Olive Young** unveiled a sharp answer right inside **Olive Young N Seongsu**: ***K-Beauty & Wellness Week***.

### Using Flagship Retail as a Cultural Gateway

Instead of waiting for tourists to visit a government information booth, KTO brought Korea's wellness and heritage map straight to the store international visitors already flock to first. Inside the Seongsu flagship, guests moved seamlessly from **personalized skin and beauty consultations** into **traditional Korean craft workshops** and **curated regional wellness itineraries** spanning temple stays, forest spas, and traditional tea houses.

### The Next Stage of Hallyu Tourism

In 2026, K-Pop concerts and K-Beauty flagships act as the front door—while Korean wellness, craftsmanship, and slow travel are becoming the living room that keeps visitors coming back for a second, third, and fourth trip to Korea.
