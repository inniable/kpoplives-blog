If late 2026 needed proof that musical identity can still outshine conglomerate marketing budgets, **RESCENE** just delivered it on Asia's biggest stages. Fresh off winning **"Best of Next"** at the **2026 MAMA Awards** in Osaka, the five-member girl group from **The Muze Entertainment** is extending a remarkable multi-win run on SBS's ***Inkigayo*** with **"With,"** the title track from their fourth mini-album ***PULSE***.

### Winning Both Critics and Broadcast Charts

For an act outside K-Pop's Big 4, scoring a single network music-show trophy is already a career-defining milestone. Stacking multiple *Inkigayo* wins in the middle of a stacked November release calendar—while simultaneously accepting a MAMA trophy at Kyocera Dome—signals that RESCENE has crossed from "hidden gem" to genuine mainstream contender.

### The "Scent & Sound" Formula

From their early sleeper hit *"LOVE ATTACK"* through *PULSE*'s *"With,"* RESCENE built an unusually cohesive aesthetic rooted in atmospheric synths, luminous vocal harmonies, and fragrance-inspired visual storytelling:

- **Consistency over gimmicks:** Every comeback reinforces a recognizable, replay-friendly sonic signature rather than pivoting wildly to chase short-form trends.
- **Organic digital legs:** High playlist retention and word-of-mouth among casual listeners power their strong digital scores on domestic music shows.
- **Global runway:** The 2026 MAMA spotlight gives RESCENE immediate visibility across Japan, Southeast Asia, and Western K-Pop communities heading into 2027.

RESCENE's November surge proves that in today's crowded 5th-generation girl-group field, unmistakable songwriting remains the ultimate equalizer.

### 대형 기획사 틈바구니에서 증명한 '음악의 힘'

- **일관된 음악 정체성:** 자극적인 숏폼 유행에 편승하기보다 반복 청취하기 좋은 완성도 높은 팝 사운드를 고수했습니다.
- **강력한 음원 롱런:** 코어 팬덤을 넘어 일반 리스너들의 플레이리스트에 안착하며 높은 음원 점수로 지상파 다관왕을 이끌었습니다.
- **글로벌 도약 발판:** 오사카 MAMA 수상으로 일본과 글로벌 팬덤 유입이 가속화되며 2027년 월드 투어·해외 페스티벌 진출 기대감도 커지고 있습니다.
