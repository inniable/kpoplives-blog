South Korea's longest-running music awards ceremony is setting its sights on **Vietnam**. Organizers of the ***2027 Golden Disc Awards*** officially unveiled the first wave of performers for January's two-night showcase at the **Vietnam Exposition Center in Hanoi**—pairing 5th-generation frontrunners with 4th-generation solo star power.

### Day 1 & Day 2 Highlights

- **Opening Night (Day 1):** Breakout rookie powerhouse **CORTIS**—riding a historic autumn that combined a Billboard 200 charting EP (*GREENGREEN*) with Song of the Year at the 2026 MAMA Awards—joins fan-favorite **ZEROBASEONE** to anchor the opening ceremony.
- **Night Two (Day 2):** **TOMORROW X TOGETHER's Yeonjun**, fresh off claiming Best Male Artist at the 2026 MAMA Awards in Osaka, will command the Hanoi stage with a dedicated solo performance.

### Why Hanoi Matters for K-Pop's 2027 Strategy

Following major K-Pop award shows in Japan, Thailand, and Indonesia, bringing the Golden Disc Awards to Hanoi's massive exposition venue reflects Vietnam's rapid rise as one of Southeast Asia's youngest, fastest-growing streaming and concert markets.
